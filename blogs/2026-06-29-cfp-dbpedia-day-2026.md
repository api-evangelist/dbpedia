The DBpedia Association is calling for presentations for DBpedia Day 2026, set for September 15 in Ghent, Belgium, co-located with SEMANTiCS 2026. Submissions on knowledge graphs and the tools, methods, and systems that support them are welcome, with a July 15, 2026 deadline. Organizers are especially interested in real-world challenges and success stories from the knowledge graph community.
